About Me

Hongcheng Li is a Postdoctoral Fellow and Assistant Researcher at the State Key Laboratory of Cyberspace Security Defense, Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences, co-advised by Prof. Weiping Wang. He received his Ph.D. degree in Cyberspace Security from the School of Cyber Security, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2026 through a direct Ph.D. program with recommended admission.

His research interests include dataset distillation, efficient inference for large language models, and secure multi-agent collaboration. Please feel free to reach out via e-mail for research discussions and potential collaborations.
